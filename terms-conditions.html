Terms & conditions

A little clarity for a thoughtful shopping experience.

Online ordering is currently unavailable. These existing store policies are provided for reference and must be confirmed before orders open.

1. Artisan Handcrafted Nature of Products

Every press-on nail set is individually sculpted, hand-painted, and detailed by Abha. Due to this handcrafted artistic process, minor variations in color gradient, marbling veins, glitter density, or brushwork may occur, ensuring your set is truly unique and one-of-a-kind. Product photos represent the design faithfully under professional studio lighting.

2. Customer Sizing & Selection Responsibility

Customers are responsible for selecting the correct shape, length, and millimeter size (XS, S, M, L, or Custom). We provide an interactive Size Calculator and standard measurement charts. Because each set is handcrafted to your exact specifications and adheres to personal hygiene standards, orders cannot be returned or refunded due to incorrect customer sizing choices.

3. Order Fulfillment & Pricing

All prices listed on nailaurabyabha.com are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are inclusive of all applicable taxes and our 6-piece nail application prep kit. We reserve the right to correct pricing errors or update collection availability at any time.

4. Safe Application & Natural Nail Care

NailAura sets are 100% non-damaging when applied and removed according to our provided care instructions. Customers must follow our gentle warm water soak-off method and avoid forcefully ripping or peeling nails off the natural nail plate.

5. Intellectual Property

All brand assets, photography, logos, copywriting, and custom nail art designs displayed on this website are the proprietary creative property of NailAura by Abha. Unauthorized commercial reproduction or imitation is prohibited.
