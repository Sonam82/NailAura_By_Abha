Privacy policy

A little clarity for a thoughtful shopping experience.

Online ordering is currently unavailable. These existing store policies are provided for reference and must be confirmed before orders open.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase a nail art set, request custom sizing, or subscribe to Aura Insiders, we only collect information necessary to fulfill your handcrafted order:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, delivery phone number, and WhatsApp number.
  • Shipping Address: Flat/house number, street address, city, state, and postal pin code.
  • Custom Sizing Specifications: Millimeter measurements submitted for bespoke nail sets.
  • Order History: Product choices, shapes, finishes, and order transaction identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to handcraft, label, and deliver your nail set; communicate order confirmations and tracking links; and provide personalized sizing assistance. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or marketing data brokers.

3. Payment Security

Payment processing is conducted via encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (UPI, Visa, Mastercard, Net Banking). NailAura does not store or process your credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account credentials on our servers.

4. Courier Sharing

To ensure timely doorstep delivery, your name, shipping address, and phone number are shared solely with verified courier partners (such as Delhivery, Blue Dart, or India Post) exclusively for transit execution and delivery SMS updates.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold or request its deletion upon order completion. For any privacy inquiries, email us directly at the contact channel listed on our contact page.
