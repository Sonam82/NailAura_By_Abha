SMALL MEASUREMENTS. A BEAUTIFUL FIT.

Made to feel like you.

A few minutes of measuring makes all the difference. Measure both hands before choosing your set.

YOUR MEASURING RITUAL

01

Tape, mark, measure.

Place a small piece of clear tape across the widest part of your natural nail. Mark both side edges, remove the tape and measure between the marks in millimetres with a ruler.

02

Do all ten.

Record thumb, index, middle, ring and pinky for each hand. Your hands may have different measurements.

03

Match your measurements.

Compare each nail with the chart. If your measurements do not match a complete standard size, choose Custom and enter both hands on the product page.
