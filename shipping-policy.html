Shipping policy

A little clarity for a thoughtful shopping experience.

Online ordering is currently unavailable. These existing store policies are provided for reference and must be confirmed before orders open.

1. Handcrafting & Studio Processing Timelines

Every press-on nail set at NailAura by Abha is individually hand-painted, structured with multiple layers of salon builder gel, and cured under UV light by Abha. Because we prioritize flawless quality and craftsmanship over factory mass production, please allow 2 to 3 business days for handcrafting, quality inspection, and packaging before dispatch.

2. Shipping Rates & Free Delivery Threshold

  • ✨ Free Pan-India Express Delivery: Automatically applied to all orders with a cart value of ₹1,999 or more.
  • 📦 Standard Flat Rate Shipping: A flat delivery fee of ₹149 applies to orders below ₹1,999.
  • ⚡ Priority Rush Handcrafting: Need your set for a wedding or event in 48 hours? WhatsApp Abha directly to check rush slot availability.

3. Estimated Transit Timelines

Once handed over to our courier partners from our studio in Mumbai, transit times depend on your delivery location:

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata): 3 to 5 business days.
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: 5 to 7 business days.
  • Special Logistics Regions (Remote or North-East zones): 7 to 9 business days.

4. Courier Tracking & Notifications

As soon as your order is packaged into our signature keepsake box and collected by the courier, the studio will need to provide a dispatch notification with a live tracking link via SMS, WhatsApp, and Email. You can follow your parcel in real-time until delivery.

5. Packaging Security & Transit Damage Guarantee

All NailAura sets are packaged inside a rigid luxury keepsake box, bubble-wrapped, and sealed in tamper-proof courier mailers. In the rare event that your package is crushed or damaged during transit, take a quick photo of the outer box and contents, and email the contact channel listed on our contact page or WhatsApp us within 48 hours. We will replace your set with zero hassle.

Have questions regarding an ongoing shipment?

Provide your Order Reference Number on our WhatsApp helpline for instant tracking assistance.
