A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Everything you might be wondering before finding your aura.

How do I choose my size?+
Measure the widest part of each natural nail and compare it with our size chart. Measure both hands; if no standard size matches, select Custom on your product page.
Can I choose a different shape?+
Yes. Each product page lists the available shapes and finishes for that design. Your chosen options stay with the set in your bag.
Can I reuse my press-ons?+
You can reuse sets that remain in good condition after gentle removal. Adhesive, wear and removal technique affect reuse. Follow the care guide and your adhesive’s directions.
How long will my set last?+
Wear time varies with preparation, adhesive choice and your routine. Follow the adhesive manufacturer’s instructions and avoid using your nails as tools.
Are the images exact product photographs?+
The collection uses AI-created design illustrations to show the style of each set. Handcrafted details and colours may vary. Final product photographs should be checked before placing an order.
Can I place an order now?+
Online ordering is not yet available. You can explore designs, choose options and save your bag. The checkout page lets you download your selection; it does not place an order or take payment.
How much is delivery?+
The current shipping policy lists ₹149 for orders below ₹1,999 and free shipping from ₹1,999. Your bag displays these estimates. See the shipping policy for details.
Can I return a custom set?+
Please read the returns and exchanges policy before ordering, particularly for custom sizing and personalised designs.
