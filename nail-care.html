PRESS. LOVE. REPEAT.

A little care.
A lot of beautiful moments.

Your guide to applying, wearing and gently removing your press-on nails.

01 / PREPARE

A clean start.

Match each press-on to its natural nail before you begin. Wash and dry your hands, gently push back cuticles and lightly buff the nail surface.

Remove dust and oils with a nail prep wipe. Let nails dry completely.

02 / APPLY

Find your fit.

Choose adhesive tabs or nail glue intended for press-ons. Follow the adhesive manufacturer’s directions for quantity, placement and holding time.

Align near the cuticle, then press down evenly. Avoid applying adhesive to the skin.

03 / ENJOY

Wear with love.

Keep your hands dry for the time recommended by your adhesive. Wear gloves for cleaning and avoid using your nails to open or pry things.

Wear time depends on preparation, adhesive and your daily routine.

04 / REMOVE & STORE

Gently does it.

Follow your adhesive’s removal instructions. Never pull or force a nail off. Give the adhesive time to release, then gently lift when loose.

Clean away residue using a method suitable for the nail material, dry fully and store in your box. Reuse only nails that remain in good condition.
