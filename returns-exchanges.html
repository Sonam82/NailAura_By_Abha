Returns & exchanges

A little clarity for a thoughtful shopping experience.

Online ordering is currently unavailable. These existing store policies are provided for reference and must be confirmed before orders open.

1. Hygiene Standards & Return Eligibility

Due to strict personal cosmetic hygiene regulations and the custom, made-to-order nature of each handcrafted set, opened, tried-on, or unwrapped nail sets cannot be returned or refunded. Because each manicure set directly adheres to human keratin, our hygienic seals cannot be re-sold once handled.

2. Transit Damage & Defective Items Guarantee

Your delight is our highest priority. If your order arrives defective, incorrect, or visibly crushed in transit, we provide a 100% complimentary replacement set:

How to Claim a Replacement:

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of package delivery.
  2. Email the contact channel listed on our contact page or WhatsApp the contact channel listed on our contact page with your Order Reference Number.
  3. Attach a clear photo or 10-second unboxing video showing the damaged box and affected nail pieces.
  4. Abha will personally review and dispatch your replacement kit within 24-48 business hours with priority shipping!

3. Sizing Accuracy & Custom Sizing Responsibility

Because nail sizes are chosen by the customer using our sizing charts or millimeter inputs, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for incorrectly chosen sizes. We strongly encourage all clients to use our Size Guide & Calculator or reach out on WhatsApp for complimentary sizing consultation prior to completing an order.

4. Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled or modified within 6 hours of order placement before Abha begins custom sizing, buffing, and gel curing. Once custom nail production has commenced, orders can no longer be cancelled.

Need Assistance with a Replacement or Order Inquiry?

Abha is available on WhatsApp to ensure your NailAura experience is magical.
